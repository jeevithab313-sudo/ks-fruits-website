About KS Fruits

Specialized Fruit Brine Processing from Bargur, Tamil Nadu

K.S. FRUITS is an established agricultural food-processing and brine-products enterprise based in Bargur, Krishnagiri District, Tamil Nadu. Operating in the heart of South India's renowned mango and citrus cultivation belt, we specialize in hygienic, natural salt-brining of green mangoes, whole lemons, and indigenous citron (Narthangai).

Our facility cures raw fruit pieces under controlled conditions with food-grade salt solution to retain firmness, natural acidity, and batch consistency for commercial pickle manufacturers, food processing plants, and culinary enterprises across India.
