About K.S. FRUITS

Rooted in Tamil Nadu's Premier Fruit Region

Located in Bargur within the Krishnagiri district of Tamil Nadu, K.S. FRUITS processes raw agricultural produce into clean, preserved fruit brine for food businesses and commercial supply.

Fresh Farm Produce

We source raw green mangoes, whole lemons, and indigenous citron (Narthangai) directly from local agricultural growers across the regional farm belt.

Traditional Brine Curing

Fruits are washed, graded, and cured using food-grade salt brine solution to retain firmness, texture, and natural characteristics for downstream food processing.

Food-Grade Storage

Brined fruits are cured and handled in dedicated food-grade storage drums and vats in an organized and monitored facility environment.
