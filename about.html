Who We Are

Fruit Processing Centered in Krishnagiri District

K.S. FRUITS operates from Bargur in Krishnagiri District, a region celebrated as Tamil Nadu's vibrant agricultural and horticultural hub. We focus on transforming raw, farm-harvested fruits into high-grade brine-cured products for commercial food industries.

By working with local farmers and agricultural suppliers, we ensure fresh procurement of raw green mangoes, lemons, and citron (Narthangai). Our process relies on traditional, chemical-free saltwater brine curation to preserve natural texture, firmness, and shelf stability for downstream manufacturing.

✓ Direct regional sourcing from Tamil Nadu agricultural belts.
✓ Dedicated brine curing using edible natural salt solutions.
✓ Commercial supply for institutional processors and food enterprises.

Operational Discipline

Principles That Guide Our Processing

We maintain an uncompromising focus on hygienic operations, natural preservation integrity, and responsive customer relations.

Hygienic Handling

Processing personnel wear protective aprons, hairnets, and gloves. All fruit washing lines, cutting stations, and curing vats undergo daily sanitation.

Purity of Ingredients

We preserve fruits solely with natural salt brine. We do not incorporate synthetic additives, artificial coloring, or spice pastes into our brine products.
