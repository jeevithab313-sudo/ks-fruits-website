Processing and Infrastructure

Raw Fruit Slicing & Crating
Initial Processing Phase

Fresh Intake, Washing & Careful Slicing

When raw mangoes, fresh lemons, or wild citrons arrive from regional farms in Krishnagiri District, they are washed and graded. Staff carefully slice and collect the firm raw fruits into clean crates ready for brine curing.

1 Inspection: Physical grading to select sound, firm fruits.
2 Water Washing: Clean washing to eliminate dust and farm residue.
3 Uniform Slicing: Sliced and sorted for consistent commercial brine batches.
Drum Salting & Curing
Preservation & Storage

Natural Salt Brine Curing & Drum Storage

The cut fruits are transferred into heavy-duty food-grade drums and layered with pure edible salt. The natural curing process ensures long-lasting shelf stability, crisp firmness, and clean preservation for commercial use.

4 Pure Salt Brining: Edible salt applied in exact ratios for stable preservation.
5 Food-Grade Drums: Cured inside sealed, heavy-duty barrels.
6 Dedicated Storage: Maintained in our Bargur facility in Krishnagiri District.
Natural Brine Vat Curing
Citrus Brine Curing

Narthangai (Citron) Salt Brine Processing

Wild citron (Narthangai) cuts are submerged in natural saltwater brine tubs. The steady curing process softens the thick citrus peel while retaining its distinctive tang and aromatic zest.

7 Zest Retention: Preserves essential citrus oils and natural peel firmness.
8 Hygienic Tubs: Processed in dedicated food-grade containers under continuous supervision.
9 Commercial Supply: Prepared for institutional buyers and traditional food processors.
Systematic Stages

The Complete Brining Cycle

Our process adheres to disciplined handling to deliver consistent quality brine batches for business clients.

01

Farm Procurement

Direct sourcing of firm raw green mangoes, lemons, and narthangai from growers in Tamil Nadu.

02

Washing & Sorting

Thorough water washing and manual sorting on hygienic stainless surfaces to ensure raw quality.

03

Preparation & Cutting

Carefully cut into uniform halves, slices, or graded whole fruits based on specific product requirements.

04

Salt Brine Mixing

Edible salt solution blended at controlled salinity to facilitate stable, natural preservation.

05

Controlled Curing

Transferred to food-grade containers where fruits undergo monitored saltwater curing to achieve desired firmness.

06

Sealed Dispatch

Securely sealed and prepared for transportation to pickle manufacturers, food factories, and bulk processors.

Hygiene & Safety Practices

Clean Facility Environment

We maintain disciplined standards to ensure safe, contaminant-free fruit processing operations.

Protective Gear

Facility personnel utilize clean aprons, hair covers, and hand protection during washing, handling, and transferring fruit batches.

Daily Floor Sanitation

Washing stations, drains, sorting conveyor tables, and floor spaces are washed and cleaned at regular intervals.

Ventilated Storage

Our Bargur warehouse is designed for optimal airflow, keeping sealed drums sheltered from direct sun and heat during the curing duration.
